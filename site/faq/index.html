WORKING WITH HELMIES

A little clarity.
Before we begin.

Scope, budgets, content, collaboration, handover, and the first conversation. Here is how to prepare for a useful start.

PROJECT QUESTIONS

From first idea
to handover.

What should I send in a first enquiry?+

A short description of the idea, who it is for, what needs to change, and any important deadline. Existing links and references are useful. You do not need a polished brief; unknowns can be discussed together.

Can a project combine several services?+

Yes. An identity, website, campaign, and connected workflow can be scoped as one coordinated project. We agree which parts are needed now and which can follow later.

How do we decide on the budget?+

We look at deliverables, complexity, content needs, integrations, and review stages before preparing a proposal. Share a range if you have one so the approach can fit the investment.

Can you give a fixed launch date?+

A target date is agreed after checking scope, availability, content readiness, and outside dependencies. Let us know what makes the date important so we can plan priorities realistically.

Do I work directly with Wael?+

Helmies is a founder-led studio. Wael connects the design and technical direction. Any additional collaborators and their responsibilities are discussed as part of the project scope.

Can you work with our existing brand or developers?+

Yes. Share current guidelines, source files, and relevant constraints. We can take responsibility for a defined part of the work and agree how reviews and handoffs fit your team.

Who supplies the website content?+

We agree this in the scope. You provide business facts and approve claims; writing, editing, photography, or asset production can be included where needed. One content owner helps keep decisions clear.

How many rounds of revisions are included?+

Review stages and revision allowances belong in the proposal. Feedback is most useful when consolidated into one response tied to the agreed goals. New directions or added deliverables may change the scope.

What do I receive at handover?+

The agreed production files, access, and usage guidance for your project. Source files, third-party licenses, account ownership, and usage rights should be specified before work begins.

Do you provide ongoing support?+

Maintenance, content updates, monitoring, and further development can be scoped separately. We clarify ongoing ownership and any support arrangement before delivery.

Can an AI project use confidential material?+

That needs a specific review of access, providers, data locations, permissions, and retention before information is connected. Start with a description of the task rather than sending sensitive records in the first email.

Will sending a brief subscribe me to marketing?+

No. The website prepares an email for you to review and send. It does not subscribe you to a mailing list or automatically submit your details.
