PRIVACY NOTICE · UPDATED 5 OCTOBER 2026

Your information.
Your choices.

This notice covers the Helmies website and enquiries you choose to send to Helmies Oy, Finland. Privacy questions and requests: info@helmies.fi.

Information & purposesStorage & providersYour rightsCookies & device storage

What happens when you visit

This website has no advertising pixels or email-open tracking pixels. Fonts, portfolio thumbnails, and the founder video are served as website assets. Portfolio links open Behance; the website does not embed its tracking widgets.

Delivering this website requires technical requests to our web host. These can include an IP address, browser information, requested address, and time. Hosting and mail providers may keep operational logs under their own arrangements. Contact Helmies for current provider and location details before sharing material with special processing requirements.

Project briefs and messages

The brief builder uses the information you type in the current page to prepare an email. It does not submit the brief to a Helmies server, save it to browser storage, or send it to an AI model. Opening the draft passes its contents to your chosen email application. You decide whether to send it.

If you email us, Helmies receives your address, the name and contact details you include, your message, and any attachments. We use this information to answer the enquiry and discuss the requested work. Taking steps at your request before a contract can provide the legal basis for that processing; responding to a business representative’s enquiry can instead rely on the legitimate interest of handling business correspondence. Information required for a resulting engagement is addressed in the project’s arrangements.

Sharing information is voluntary, but we need a way to reply and enough context to understand your request. Please do not include passwords, payment-card information, or sensitive personal records in an initial brief. An early-access email is an enquiry, not an automatic newsletter subscription.

Who handles information

Your chosen email provider handles draft creation and delivery. The Helmies mailbox provider handles receipt and outgoing mail; the private studio dashboard can keep a copy of synchronized message text and messages sent through it. Only signed-in administrators can access that dashboard.

Provider locations, contractual safeguards, and retention arrangements can differ between services. This notice does not make an EU-only hosting promise. Contact us before sending material that needs a specific processing location or a data-processing agreement, so the relevant providers and any international-transfer safeguards can be confirmed for the proposed work.

How long information is kept

A brief exists in the current page until you leave or reset it; your browser may restore a page during a browsing session. A copied brief remains in your device clipboard until replaced or cleared. Once an email is sent, it becomes correspondence rather than temporary website input.

The appropriate retention of correspondence depends on whether an enquiry remains active, becomes a project, or must be retained for a legal obligation or a claim. Ask us about the retention or deletion of a specific message. This page does not promise an automatic mailbox deletion schedule that the website cannot enforce.

Device-storage lifetimes and deletion controls are listed on the cookies and device storage page.

Your GDPR rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. These rights have conditions and exceptions.

Email info@helmies.fi with your request. Include enough information to locate the relevant correspondence; avoid sending identity documents unless needed and requested. GDPR requests generally require a response within one month, with a possible extension for complex or numerous requests.

You may lodge a complaint with Finland’s Data Protection Ombudsman or another competent supervisory authority. Read the European Commission’s explanation of individual rights.

Automated decisions and AI

The website does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about visitors. Public search and the service navigator run against website information. In the private dashboard, AI can help prepare articles and business email drafts using the selected text and prospect observations. AI output is reviewed before publication or sending. Your public project brief is not automatically submitted to an AI model.

Changes and project-specific information

This notice describes this website. A future product, client project, newsletter, or new integration may require additional information and choices before it is used. The date above identifies this revision.

Aggregate website analytics

When enabled, the website counts public page requests by day, page path, referring hostname, and broad device category. These aggregate counters do not store IP addresses, full referrer URLs, query strings, cookies, or unique visitor identifiers. Reloads count separately. Known crawlers, administrator visits, and requests expressing Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are excluded. The default retention is 90 days; contact us for the current configured period. Separate hosting logs are outside these counters.

Business enquiries and outreach

For business prospect research, the private dashboard can retain a business name, website, publicly listed business contact address, the page where it was found, technical website observations, and review notes. Public availability of an address does not by itself establish permission to market to it. Contact eligibility and relevance are reviewed before an offer is approved. A drafted offer is not sent automatically by the researcher.

If you receive an offer, you can use its unsubscribe link or reply to ask us to stop. The dashboard records an exclusion to prevent further campaign emails. Contact us about the source, use, correction, or deletion of a prospect record. Retention of outreach and correspondence records is reviewed according to their purpose and any applicable obligations; an unsubscribe record may be retained to respect your request.

Private dashboard sessions

Only administrators sign in to the studio dashboard. Its necessary session cookie, helmies_admin, expires after 12 hours. It is HttpOnly and uses secure transport on the production HTTPS site. Logout revokes the session. Public visitors do not need an administrator account.
