COOKIES & DEVICE STORAGE · UPDATED 5 OCTOBER 2026

A clear view
of what stays.

Helmies’ website code sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses a few browser preferences and offers optional offline storage. You can read the site without enabling offline storage.

Offline pages

If enabled, a service worker saves public pages and assets on this device for repeat visits without a connection. It does not save your brief, and it does not cache the founder video. Turning it off removes the Helmies offline cache.

Device tilt and motion

Device tilt is off until you enable it. Where required, the browser asks for sensor permission. Orientation readings move decorative visuals in the page; the website does not send them to Helmies. Use the footer controls to turn tilt or motion off. The site also respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.

Hosting and external services

This inventory covers storage created by Helmies website code. Browser caching and the web host operate separately. External services, including your email app and Behance after you follow a link, have their own settings and policies. The private administration dashboard uses a necessary session cookie only after an administrator signs in.

Reset your website preferences

This removes the Helmies motion and announcement preferences, disables offline storage, and clears its saved public pages on this device. It does not delete sent emails, clipboard content, or data held by external providers.

You can also remove site data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not send a deletion request to Helmies.

About consent

Optional storage should follow a clear, freely made choice that can be withdrawn. Our offline controls let you enable or disable this feature without blocking access to the site. See Traficom’s guidance on cookies and similar technologies.
