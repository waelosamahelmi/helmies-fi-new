A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
we begin.

Can you redesign our current website?+

Yes. We review what should be retained, what is missing, and how existing URLs and content should be handled.

Do you include SEO?+

We can include content structure and technical SEO foundations. Search performance also depends on competition, content quality, reputation, and ongoing work; rankings are not guaranteed.

Can the website be multilingual?+

Yes. We can plan language-specific content, navigation, metadata, and review as part of the project scope.

MAKE THE FIRST CONVERSATION COUNTYOUR STARTING POINT

Bring the context.

Tell us who the work is for, what is difficult today, and what a useful change would look like. Share relevant existing material so we can understand the starting point.

Choose the first outcome.

Plan for real use.

Who will approve the work, keep it current, and use the handover? Clarifying those roles helps the result become part of your everyday business.
