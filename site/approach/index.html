HOW WE WORK

Room for wonder.
A plan to get there.

A collaborative process that turns an ambitious idea into something you can see, use, and share.

01

DISCOVER

Start with the right question.

We listen to the ambition, look at the context, and define the problem worth solving. We agree on the audience, the scope, and what a useful result should look like.

  • Discovery conversation
  • Scope & priorities
  • Success criteria
02

IMAGINE

Make the direction tangible.

A prototype, a moodboard, a storyboard, or a proof of concept. We create something concrete enough to react to before the project moves into full production.

  • Creative direction
  • Early prototype
  • Feedback & alignment
03

CREATE

Build with the details in mind.

Design and production move in reviewable stages. We share progress, test the important parts, and refine the work around the agreed purpose.

  • Design & production
  • Milestone reviews
  • Testing & refinement
04

EVOLVE

Give the launch a future.

We prepare the agreed deliverables, clarify how they should be used, and plan the next chapter. Maintenance and further development can be scoped around the needs of the project.

  • Launch preparation
  • Handover & guidance
  • Next-step planning

WORKING TOGETHER

Clear from
the beginning.

How do we start?+

Share a short brief or email us. We’ll discuss the idea and shape an appropriate next step.

How are budget and timing decided?+

We review the scope, complexity, content, and dependencies before agreeing on a proposal. No fixed timeline fits every type of work.

Can you collaborate with our team?+

Yes. We can take on a defined part of a project or bring several disciplines together, with responsibilities agreed in advance.

What happens after delivery?+

We agree on handover, documentation, and any support needs as part of the scope. Ongoing maintenance and development can be planned separately.
